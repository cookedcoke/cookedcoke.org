Three rules we don't break.

Menu

Healthy first

Everything on the shelf is checked against the school-healthy menu before we order it. We lead with low-sugar and zero-sugar lines.

Stock

No overlap with the canteen

We don't stock anything the school canteen sells. We run alongside it, never in competition with it.

Money

Every sale on the record

All takings go through Square. No cash drawer, no untracked sales, so the books can be checked at any time.
